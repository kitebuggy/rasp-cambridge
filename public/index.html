Subscribe to the calendar from your calendar app:

webcal://kitebuggy.github.io/rasp-cambridge/cambridge_rasp.ics

Or download the latest ICS: cambridge_rasp.ics

Audit-trail PNG charts are in /charts.

Build state (which days RASP served on the last run): build_state.json.

Regenerated at 05:00, 08:00 and 10:00 UTC via GitHub Actions, then rechecked hourly to 17:00 UTC until all seven days are available. Days RASP cannot serve appear as "no data" placeholders - never as an out-of-date rating.
