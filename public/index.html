Build state (which days RASP served on the last run): build_state.json.

Regenerated at 05:23, 08:23 and 10:23 UTC via GitHub Actions, then rechecked hourly at :23 past until 17:23 UTC, or until all seven days are available. Days RASP cannot serve appear as "no data" placeholders - never as an out-of-date rating.
